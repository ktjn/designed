# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Designed a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, concisely, and respectfully.
- Focus feedback on technical merit, accessibility, and user impact.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or disruptive conduct in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or restrict participation as needed to uphold these standards.
